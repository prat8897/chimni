# Code of Conduct

Chimni is a small project where people argue about language design. Arguments are welcome; hostility is not.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good faith. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be specific and concrete when you disagree. "Here's a program this breaks" beats "this is wrong."
- Respect that people have different experience levels, backgrounds and first languages.

## What we won't accept

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Doxxing or sharing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome attention directed at a person.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close or lock threads, and remove people from the project's spaces
when behaviour breaks these expectations. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on
their GitHub profile; reports are handled in confidence.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run by the project.
